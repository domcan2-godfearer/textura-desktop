YOUR FIRST SESSION

Download. Install.
Open the text.

TEXTURA includes the resources; Library prepares them for use in your local study profile.

  1. 1

    Install your desktop edition

    On Windows, run the setup EXE. On Ubuntu, open a terminal in the download folder and run:

    sudo apt install ./textura-desktop_1.0.4_all.deb
  2. 2

    Choose your resources

    BSB, KJV, and commentaries prepare on first launch. In Library, install other translations and the Greek resources you want to use. Hebrew is a separate optional resource, about 7.8 MB compressed. Included resource installation works offline.

  3. 3

    Begin with a passage

    Select a book and chapter. Click a verse to connect its Greek wording, commentary, cross-references, and your own notes.

A note on original language sources

The Greek inspector uses SBL / Macula for the New Testament and Swete for the Septuagint, with Theodotion for Daniel. It does not represent every Majority Standard Bible reading. Ecclesiastes Greek is absent from the supplied source. Provisional grammar and counterpart alignments retain their qualifications and source notices inside the app. A shared lemma alone does not establish the same meaning, a quotation, or a direct textual connection.

Hebrew: WLC / Macula, Open Scriptures morphology, Cherith English glosses, and STEPBible TVTMS numbering. Includes biblical Aramaic. Glosses are reading aids, not full dictionary definitions; source annotations and extended Strong’s identifiers retain their qualifications in the app.

Where are my notes stored?

Notes and bookmarks stay in your local desktop profile. Windows, Ubuntu, and the web reader use separate profiles. In Notes, choose Export complete study backup; restore that backup on the other computer to transfer notes, bookmarks, and reading preferences.

Does it need the website to run?

No. The desktop editions use the resources bundled with the application. External source links open in your browser and need an internet connection.
